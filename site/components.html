Carousel

.os-carousel

The pictures of one item, one per view on a sunken background. Swipe on touch screens; ‹ › buttons, the counter, arrow keys and Home/End come from oldschool.js. Thumbnails are links to the slides, so it all works without JavaScript. Use <img> in the slides (shown with object-fit: contain) and loading="lazy" on every picture but the first; the demo uses drawn placeholders because the showcase has no photos.

Buttons

.os-button .os-button--retro

Flat: a solid primary, a white secondary with a rule, a bold label. 30px tall; 40px on touch screens. One primary per view. .os-button--ghost is a button without a box, for "··· More".

View

Retro style, the 2008 button: a top-to-bottom gradient. The retro theme uses it everywhere; elsewhere use .os-button--retro on a button, or data-buttons="retro" on any element (or <html>) for every button inside. --flat and data-buttons="flat" switch back. The nearest setting wins; sizes, icons and groups work in both styles.

Notification

.os-notification Oldschool.notify()

The small window that slides up in the bottom corner when something has happened — a title bar in the tone's colour, a close box, a line of text and an optional link. It stays while the pointer or focus is inside; Escape closes it. placement: 'center' puts it in the middle of the screen over a dimmed page, for the one confirmation that matters.
