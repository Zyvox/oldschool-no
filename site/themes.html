Themes

A theme is two ramps — an accent and a neutral — plus optional overrides. Light and dark are not separate themes: every theme gets both through light-dark() and the inherited color-scheme. And because tokens re-resolve on any element with data-theme, themes can be scoped to a single box, as every card below is.

<html data-theme="furu" data-mode="dark" data-density="compact">
<section data-theme="retro"> … one retro island … </section>
